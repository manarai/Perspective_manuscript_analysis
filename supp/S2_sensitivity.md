# Supplementary Table S2 — Metric-sensitivity sweep

**Protocol.** Every twelve-computation entry is re-evaluated under three per-state rescalings of the Jacobian: uniform ×0.5, uniform ×2.0, and a per-state draw from Uniform(0.5, 2.0) with a fixed seed (numpy.random.default_rng(0)). Baseline is the descriptor at the stated operating point; min / max span the values across the three rescalings.

**Reading.** Q is invariant under change of representation and is not tabulated. ν, ω and G_max are metric-relative. Two systems have ω crossing zero under some rescaling (symmetric repressilator, Goodwin) — these are the two amber points in Figure 2a. NF-κB sits close to the zero boundary at ω = +0.009 but does not cross (minimum +0.001). Selkov's ω stays positive throughout (min +0.294).
